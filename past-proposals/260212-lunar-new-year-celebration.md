## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Lunar New Year Celebration & Alumni Mixer
**Date:** Thursday, February 12, 2026, 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM
**Venue:** MScAC Office 9014 (Main Collaborative Space)
**Estimated Attendance:** 35–40 (20–25 current students + 15 alumni/seniors)

---

### **Purpose**

To celebrate the cultural heritage of the significant East and Southeast Asian diaspora within the MScAC cohort and foster community across year groups. Following the high interest shown in preliminary Discord polling, this event serves as both a cultural milestone and a networking opportunity. By inviting alumni back to the office for traditional festivities like the Lo Hei (Prosperity Toss), we aim to strengthen the bond between current students and the professional alumni network in a relaxed, festive environment.

### **Event Components**

**Catering & Lo Hei:**

* A full Chinese dinner featuring auspicious dishes (dumplings, noodles, and fish). The centerpiece will be the Lo Hei (Singaporean/Malaysian prosperity toss), a collaborative activity where participants toss ingredients together to welcome good fortune for the coming year.

**Festive Snacks:**

* Sourced from ShopMalaysia Canada.

**Dessert from The Sweet Spot:**

* Bing Fen and other traditional Asian desserts for all attendees to conclude the meal on a sweet, festive note.

**Karaoke Station:**

* A dedicated area in MScAC 9014 for students and alumni to engage in one of the most popular social pastimes within the diaspora.

**Themed Door Gifts:**

* "Hongbao" (red envelopes) containing small themed items or vouchers, and Year of the Horse-themed memorabilia to commemorate the 2026 Lunar New Year.
