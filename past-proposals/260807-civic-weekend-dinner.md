## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Civic Weekend Dinner — A Celebration of Where We All Came From
**Date:** Friday, August 7, 2026, 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM
**Venue:** MScAC Office 9014 (Main Collaborative Space)
**Estimated Attendance:** 50 (current + incoming students, plus alumni/seniors)

---

### **Purpose**

To close out the Civic Holiday weekend with a dinner celebrating the diversity of backgrounds that make up the MScAC community — a lighthearted nod to Canadian civic pride paired with an appreciation of how far cohort members have traveled, literally and figuratively, to get here. This continues a format the fund has already backed twice this year: the Lunar New Year Celebration & Alumni Mixer ($2,315) and the Drop-in Wellness Café ($1,250) both invited alumni and seniors alongside current students, and both were approved specifically because that mix strengthens mentorship and cohort spirit. Several incoming students are already in Toronto and have been joining our current game nights; this dinner keeps that momentum visible and sets the tone of consistent community programming the incoming cohort will step into at the end of August.

### **Event Components**

**Dinner & Social (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM):**

* **Catering:** A shared, family-style dinner drawing on the range of cuisines represented in the cohort — reflecting the "how we all got here" theme rather than any single cuisine.
* **Refreshments:** Non-alcoholic beverages (iced tea, sodas, water) available throughout.
* **Alumni & Incoming Student Mixing:** No formal programming — alumni, current, and incoming students are seated and encouraged to mix, continuing the informal-mentorship pattern from the Wellness Café and CNY events.

**Dessert & Wrap-Up (8:00 PM – 9:00 PM):**

* **Dessert:** A shared dessert spread to close the evening.
* **Informal Sharing:** Optional, low-pressure moment for a few attendees (alumni and students) to share a short story of their own path to Toronto/MScAC, tying back to the event's theme.
