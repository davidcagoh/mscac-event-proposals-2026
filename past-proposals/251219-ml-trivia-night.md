## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Machine Learning Trivia Night with Dinner
**Date:** Friday, December 19, 2025, 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM
**Venue:** MScAC Room 9016
**Estimated Attendance:** 30

---

### **Purpose**

This initiative serves two primary objectives: to foster stronger social cohesion and bonding among the cohort during the winter period, and to provide a fun, engaging way to promote recruiting preparation by refreshing students' knowledge of core Machine Learning concepts ahead of the winter/spring recruiting cycle. The collaborative trivia format will encourage informal, low-stakes knowledge sharing.

### **Event Components**

**Dinner (6:30 PM – 7:15 PM):**

* A catered dinner will be served buffet/family-style to facilitate casual networking and allow students to arrive and settle in comfortably before the main activity.

**Trivia Competition (7:15 PM – 8:15 PM):**

* Students will pre-register/self-select into teams of 4–5 to compete in a multi-round ML trivia competition. Questions will cover a range of ML/CS fundamentals, recent advancements, and applied concepts relevant to internship interviews.

**Prize Ceremony and Social Hour (8:15 PM – 9:00 PM):**

* A brief prize-giving ceremony will recognize the top two teams. The room will remain booked until 9:00 PM to allow attendees to finish food, play supplementary board games, and continue socializing.
