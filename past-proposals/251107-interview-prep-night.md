## **MScAC Initiatives Proposal (Revised)**

**Event:** Interview Prep Night with Food
**Date:** Thursday, November 7, 2025
**Venue:** MScAC Room 9016
**Estimated Attendance:** Dinner for 30–40; drinks and snacks for up to 80

---

### **Purpose**

Responding to high student demand for collaborative internship preparation. Students benefit from peer prep significantly more than solo preparation, as evidenced by weekly Tuesday morning prep sessions. A social, structured event allows more students to participate and gain actionable feedback.

### **Event Components**

**Food Area:**

* Medium trays from Mr Yes, served family-style (~5 servings per tray). Non-alcoholic drinks also provided, allowing total attendance up to 80.

**Prep Stations / Clusters:**

* **Resume Peer Evaluation:** Participants give and receive feedback on resumes.
* **Technical Interview Stations:** Coding/problem-solving practice along the large whiteboard.
* **Behavioural Interview Mock Activity:** Paired roleplay and scenario practice.
* **ML Foundations Discussion:** Group discussions on key ML concepts relevant to internships.
* **Networking / Socializing:** Layout encourages interaction between students who don't usually collaborate, fostering peer learning and broader connections.
