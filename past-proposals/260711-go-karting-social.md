## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Go-Karting Social @ K1 Speed Toronto
**Date:** Saturday, July 11, 2026, 3:00 PM
**Venue:** K1 Speed Toronto (external)
**Estimated Attendance:** 18 students (target), 14 confirmed

---

### **Purpose**

Go-karting was proposed organically by a student and immediately drew strong cohort interest (14 confirmed interested attendees so far). However, recognizing the cost barrier to students, this proposal requests a partial participation subsidy of $50 per student to ensure the event remains accessible to everyone who wants to join, regardless of financial circumstance. By absorbing a portion of the per-person cost, the program enables full cohort participation in a high-energy, off-campus experience that is meaningfully different from our usual on-campus socials.

### **Event Components**

**Go-Karting Races (3:00 PM onwards):**

* The group will race at K1 Speed Toronto, one of the city's indoor karting venues. 
* Students may individually choose their race package: 2 races ($71) or 3 races ($88). The $50 subsidy applies regardless of the package chosen. If more students are able to join us, the subsidy will be split equally among all attendees.
* Hassan and David will coordinate arrival, manage the group booking, and handle payment on behalf of all attendees.
* All participants are required to bring a government-issued ID to race.

**Post-Race Social:**

* Following the races, the group will have the opportunity to debrief and socialize over dinner at their own expense.
