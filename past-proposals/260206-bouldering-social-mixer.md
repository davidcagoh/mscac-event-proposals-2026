## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Bouldering & Social Mixer   
**Date:** Friday, February 6, 2026, 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM   
**Venue:** Basecamp Climbing (Queen Street) & Cheng Du Street Food (Local Restaurant)   
**Estimated Attendance:** 15 (early registration is already 12 pax)  
---

### **Purpose**

To promote physical wellness, team building, and stress relief within the MScAC cohort. Bouldering is a uniquely collaborative sport that encourages peers to solve "problems" (climbing routes) together, mirroring the problem-solving nature of the program. This outing aims to foster organic networking and mentorship, as several current MScAC students who are already gym members will guide newcomers through safety protocols and climbing techniques in a supportive environment.

### **Event Components**

**Climbing Session (3:00 PM – 6:00 PM):**

* **Safety Orientation:** All first-time climbers will undergo a mandatory site briefing provided by Basecamp staff to ensure compliance with gym safety regulations.  
* **Peer Mentorship:** Experienced climbers within the cohort will act as informal guides to help beginners navigate the gym layout and basic bouldering movements.  
* **Equipment:** Provision of professional shoe rentals for all attendees to ensure a safe and standardized experience.

**Post-Climb Dinner (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM):**

* **Social Connection:** Since food and drink are prohibited on the climbing floor, a dedicated dinner session at a nearby eatery (Cheng Du Street Food, see quotes) will allow for a more relaxed environment to facilitate deeper social bonds.  
* **Dietary Accommodations:** Budgeting accounts for a variety of meal options (including vegetarian, vegan, and halal) to ensure all participants are included.

### **RSVP Plan**

* **Coordination:** RSVPs will be collected via the MScAC Discord/Google Forms, shoe size confirmed onsite.   
* **Confirmation:** A final headcount will be established by February 4th to finalize dinner reservations and notify Basecamp of the group size.

**Contingency Plan**

* In the event that the proposal cannot be approved in time, **Friday 13 February 2026** is the backup date as there are almost as many poll respondents preferring that date. 

### **Other Next Steps**

* **Waiver Management:** Ensure all participants sign the online Basecamp liability waiver prior to arrival, as well as MScAC liability waiver (used previously)  
* **Venue Booking:** Confirm a dinner reservation for 15 people at a restaurant within walking distance of Queen St.  
* **Administrative Follow-up:** Collect and submit all itemized receipts, the final attendee list, and a copy of the invitation for reimbursement.
