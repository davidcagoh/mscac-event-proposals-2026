## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** FIFA World Cup 2026 Final Watch Party
**Date:** Sunday, July 19, 2026, 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM
**Venue:** MScAC Room 9014 (match viewing may instead be held at a public outdoor screening at Nathan Phillips Square, to be confirmed closer to the date), followed by dinner at MScAC Room 9014
**Estimated Attendance:** 20–25 students

---

### **Purpose**

The FIFA World Cup 2026 Final is one of the most-watched sporting events in the world, and with the tournament being hosted on home soil for the first time in decades, interest across the cohort is exceptionally high. This event gives the MScAC community a dedicated, comfortable space to experience the match together — turning what would otherwise be a solo viewing experience into a shared moment for the cohort. Following prior successful watch parties in Room 9014, this format has proven itself as one of the most natural and inclusive social formats the program offers, welcoming students regardless of how closely they follow the sport. Capping off the day with dinner back at Room 9014 extends the celebration into a full cohort social rather than a match-length event.

### **Event Components**

**Match Viewing (3:00 PM – ~5:00 PM):**

* Full match broadcast either on Room 9014's projection system with integrated audio, or as a group outing to a public outdoor screening (e.g. Nathan Phillips Square) — final call to be made closer to the date depending on cohort preference and screening availability.
* Sander and David will manage hosting/coordination duties, including AV troubleshooting if the viewing stays in-room.
* Half-time break used as a natural social window — students encouraged to mix and decompress.

**Dinner & Social (~5:00 PM – 10:00 PM):**

* The cohort regroups at MScAC Room 9014 for dinner and continued socializing following the match, regardless of where it was watched.
* Food will be laid out buffet-style, with finger foods and beverages available throughout the evening.
* Casual wind-down as the evening winds on. Room cleared and returned to standard setup by 10:00 PM.
