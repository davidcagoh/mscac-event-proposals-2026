## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Poker Social  
**Date:**, Friday, 27 Feb 2026, 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM  
**Venue:** MScAC Room 9016  
**Estimated Attendance:** 30 players \+ \~10 spectators  
---

### **Purpose**

To foster community engagement and strategic networking through a structured, high-energy social event. Following a busy academic period, this event provides a platform for students to connect outside of a technical environment. By leveraging the expertise of student organizers, we aim to provide a flexible tournament experience that builds camaraderie across the cohort, catering to both seasoned players and casual beginners.

### **Event Components**

**Poker Play & Optionality (3:00 PM – 6:00 PM):**

* **Dual-Track Format:** To maximize inclusivity, the room will be split into two modes of play based on RSVP interest:  
  * **Competitive Bracket:** A structured "Sit-and-Go" tournament for experienced players.  
  * **Casual/Learning Tables:** A "cash-style" format (using play chips) allowing for flexible buy-ins/outs, ideal for those wanting to learn the game without the pressure of a tournament clock.  
* **Table Logistics:** Utilization of 5 poker sets (all sourced from the office/students). 2–3 sets will be dedicated to each mode of play depending on final registration numbers.  
* **Facilitation:** Kevin, Sander, and Tom will act as directors, managing blind structures for the tournament track and providing rule guidance for the casual track.

**Dinner & Social (6:00 PM – 7:00 PM):**

* **Catering:** Intermission dinner featuring a variety of options from local vendors (e.g., Pizza or Asian catering), served family-style.  
* **Refreshments:** A selection of non-alcoholic beverages and light snacks available throughout the day.

**Tournament Finals & Open Play (7:00 PM – 9:00 PM):**

* **Main Event Final Table:** The top players from the competitive bracket move to a single "Final Table" with spectators invited to watch.  
* **Consolation Tables:** Casual play continues on secondary tables for those eliminated from the main tournament.
