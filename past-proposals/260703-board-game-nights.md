## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** MScAC Board Game Nights (July Series)

**Estimated Attendance:** ~20 students per session

*Revised resubmission: the July 17 session has been removed and the budget reduced accordingly, per MScAC staff's request to avoid overlap with the FIFA World Cup Final Watch Party week.*

---

### **Purpose**

Board game nights have become one of the cohort's most consistent and well-attended social traditions — sessions organized throughout the year have reliably drawn 20 or more students every time, without formal promotion. This proposal formalizes that momentum into a structured four-week summer series, giving the cohort a regular, low-pressure space to unwind, connect across streams, and build the kind of ongoing camaraderie that a single one-off event cannot. The series format is intentional: community deepens with repetition, and a predictable weekly slot lowers the barrier for students who missed a prior session to show up the next week. The July 17 session is intentionally omitted to avoid conflicting with the cohort's FIFA World Cup Final Watch Party the same week.

### **Event Components**

**Open Play (6:00 PM – 9:30 PM):**

* Multiple games run simultaneously across the room, drawing from the cohort's existing library of board games. Tables self-organize by interest and experience level, keeping the format welcoming to first-timers and regulars alike.
* Sander, Hassan, Oleksii, Cosmo, and David will rotate facilitation duties — explaining rules, balancing table sizes, and keeping energy up across sessions.
* A new game, selected by cohort poll prior to the series launch, will be introduced in the first session to refresh the rotation and bring new energy to regulars.

**Wind-Down & Social (9:30 PM – 10:00 PM):**

* Games wrap up and the room shifts to casual conversation. Light snacks and drinks remain available.
* Organizers collect informal feedback on game preferences to inform future session planning.
