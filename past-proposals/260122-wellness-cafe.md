## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Drop-in Wellness Café
**Date:** Thursday, January 22, 2026, 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM
**Venue:** MScAC Office (large common room)
**Estimated Attendance:** 20–25 (20 current students, plus additional senior students and alumni dropping in during the evening)

---

### **Purpose**

This event is designed to support MScAC students during the peak of internship recruiting season, a period that is often marked by heightened stress, isolation, and burnout. Rather than a structured workshop, the Drop-in Wellness Café provides a low-pressure, welcoming environment where students can decompress, work quietly, or connect socially. By extending the event into the evening and inviting seniors and alumni after the end of their workday, the café also creates organic opportunities for mentorship, informal advice, and reassurance from those who have recently navigated similar recruiting cycles.

### **Event Components**

**Wellness Café (3:00 PM – 6:00 PM):**

* The afternoon portion will emphasize calm, flexible use of space. The room will be arranged with tables and soft seating to allow students to sit alone or in small groups at low volume.
* **Beverages:** Hot tea (assorted bags sourced from the MScAC pantry), iced tea, isotonic drinks, and bottled water.
* **Snacks:** Individually portioned, easy-to-eat items such as MadeGood granola bites, fruit (bananas, apples, clementines), and light packaged snacks.
* **Quiet Space:** A small beanbag and book/reading corner to encourage rest and decompression alongside light independent work.

**Dinner & Evening Drop-in (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM):**

* The evening segment will be more social while maintaining a relaxed atmosphere.
* **Dinner:** A mix of finger foods to allow for easy grazing and conversation, including pizza and small-scale Asian catering (e.g., dumplings, noodles, or rice-based trays).
* **Networking:** Seniors and alumni are invited to drop in, eat, and casually connect with current students. No formal programming is planned, keeping interactions natural and student-driven.
* **Flow:** Food will be served buffet-style, allowing attendees to come and go without disruption.
