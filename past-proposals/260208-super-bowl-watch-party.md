## **MScAC Initiative Budget Proposal**

**Event:** Super Bowl LX Office Watch Party   
**Date:** Sunday, February 8, 2026, 6:00 PM to 10:30 PM   
**Venue:** MScAC Room 9014   
**Estimated Attendance:** 20–25 students

### **Purpose**

This event is designed to foster community spirit and provide a much-needed social break for the cohort during the winter term. The Super Bowl is a significant cultural touchpoint that offers an ideal backdrop for peer bonding outside of an academic context. Interest in this initiative is exceptionally high; a recent poll garnered 15 "Yes" votes within the first two hours, indicating a strong desire for a communal viewing experience within the MScAC space.

### **Event Components**

* **Catering (Game Day Spread):** A variety of premium finger foods including chicken wings, sliders, vegetarian spring rolls, and nachos. Non-alcoholic beverages (sparkling water, sodas, and juice) will be provided. Food is scheduled for delivery at 6:15 PM to ensure it is fresh for kickoff.  
* **AV & Cinematic Setup:** Full utilization of the Room 9014 projection system and integrated sound. The setup will be tested at 6 PM to ensure high-definition streaming and immersive audio for the group.  
* **Social Engagement:** While the game is the focus, the room will be organized with a dedicated "half-time social" area to allow students to network (and discuss internship progress?\!) during breaks.  
* **Interactive Elements:** To keep the atmosphere inclusive for those less familiar with American football, activities like "Super Bowl Bingo" cards centered on the commercials and halftime show will be organized. 

### **RSVP Plan**

* RSVPs and specific dietary requirements (Halal, Vegan, Gluten-Free) will be tracked via a cohort form.  
* Final food quantities will be adjusted 48 hours prior to the event based on the confirmed attendee list to minimize waste.

**Other Next Steps**

* Confirm weekend building access (Sunday) and room booking for Room 9014\.  
* Designate two student leads to manage food arrival and tech troubleshooting.
